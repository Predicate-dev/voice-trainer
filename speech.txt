I'm excited to present my idea for Phase Two: The Beat, a voice-based AI coach 
designed to help users master the art of public speaking by providing real-time, 
non-intrusive feedback on pacing, volume, and vocal tone.

The problem I'm tackling is the common struggle for both novice and experienced 
speakers who, when nervous or unprepared, lose control of their delivery. They 
speak too quickly, fail to project their voice, and deliver a monotone presentation, 
all of which erode clarity, impact, and confidence. Existing tools provide feedback 
after a session, but they fail to provide the real-time, in-the-moment correction 
and guidance that's necessary to build muscle memory and improve on the spot.
